The Background

A career built where the stakes were highest.

Manny Engel has spent more than two decades building and leading security programs across the full spectrum of business — from early-stage startups to mid-market companies to Fortune 500 enterprises. He has served as a Chief Information Security Officer and VP-level security leader, building programs from the ground up and leading the response when major breach events put everything on the line.

That range matters. Manny has sat in the boardroom translating technical risk into business decisions, and he has been in the war room at 2 a.m. when an incident was unfolding. He understands what it takes to protect an organization not in theory, but from having done it — repeatedly, under pressure, with real consequences.

Before his civilian career, Manny served in the United States Marine Corps. The discipline, accountability, and mission-first mindset he carried out of the Corps still define how Veritas operates: clear objectives, honest assessments, and ownership of the outcome.

Today, Veritas channels all of that experience toward the businesses that need it most — the growing and mid-market companies that face the same threats as the Fortune 500 but have never had a security executive of their own. That's the gap Veritas was built to close.
